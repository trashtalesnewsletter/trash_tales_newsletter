Monday

Once I finished raising the roof(lines) I met up with the same childhood friend I caught up with last week. Along with him was his cousin who I’ve also known since childhood and her son who is in the 8th grade. The three of them haven’t spent a lot of time in SF so I thought it would be fun to show them Twin Peaks.

I was reminded just how much of a child my childhood friend was in the best way possible. He was so excited to climb, turn over stones to try to find lizards and generally was just really enthusiastic which I really appreciated.

When I told him about the times I would run with “Franklin” he said “You really just do whatever don’t you?” That’s funny coming from the guy who swings clubs around and went to K2 basecamp but you know. Maybe it takes one to know one.

Tuesday

After graphicsing all my processing units I went to play Spikeball with "The Spikeballer” and a bunch of the other panhandle folks. I didn’t really stick around for long today because I had more processing units to graphics up but I did finally form up with a nickname for someone I wanted to for a little bit.

There was a guy there who I just met recently and is on the newsletter but I couldn’t come up with a name for. I farmed for fun facts until learning he’s Jewish from which I derived “This guy doesn’t get passed over”.

Wednesday

Upon finishing another hunt in the bazel mines I met up with “The Raja of rhythm” and a couple of his other friends for drinks.

We started off at a bar in South Mission and found overselves over in Nob hill. The Nob hill bar was full of crowded yuppies and I’m not sure how to feel about that.

Thursday

While I was in the middle of shaking the rust off the compiler a separate childhood friend from above reached out saying he was in SF and wanted to know when I was free. Our schedules didn’t align except for lunch today so we met up for lunch at Anatolian table.

He’s someone I’ve known since the 3rd grade so a good amount of our catch up was reminiscing and me learning how other people from our high school are doing.

Excitingly, he finished his PhD in neuroscience and is now a postdoc.

We didn’t catch up for long but it was great to see him again.

Once I finished shaking the rust off the compiler I went to bar night with “The Mosher” and “That’s nuts”.

“That’s nuts” is trying to come up with a joke birthday present for her boyfriend “The Orchestra”. She said he would buy anything with Mamdani’s face on them so she wanted to put them somewhere funny. I suggested a phone case for something more permanent and condoms for something more ephemeral. I also thought the inside of his shoes could be good as well. If any of you have any fun ideas for where to put Mandan stickers let me know. Fortunately “The Orchestra” doesn’t get the newsletter so this will still be surprise to him :D

I had an assignment to help "The Mosher” with his dating profile so I spent some time refining his prompts and getting him to actually launch the profile.

The launch itself was great! Within a minute he already had a like on his profile. Granted that like may have come from a guy but still.

The newsletter came in unexpectedly handy when we debated when me and “Hand in hand” did the bit where we held hands. “That’s nuts" thought it happened at her birthday party while I contended it happened at “TheJuggler’s”. I pulled up the archive and I was in fact right. Let that be a lesson munchkins; if you write a newsletter you can use it to prove when things happened in your life!

Afterwards we went to Lily Coits to meet up with “The Philosopher,” “The Awepartment,” “The Juggler,” “The Thunder from down under” and a few others. Last week I told you all I would challenge “That’s nuts” to an oyster eating competition and here we are. Earlier in the day I found out that “The Juggler” wanted to join in. I figured I’d have a reasonable chance of beating “That’s nuts” but “The Juggler” is an entirely different story. They don’t call him the mountain eater for nothing (I hope you appreciate the One Piece reference “La Taqueria Royalty” and “The Writer”). “The Mosher” also wanted to join the competition but I wasn’t as worried about him beating me compared to “The Juggler”

I think the competition was supposed to be that we would all eat oysters together until we dropped off but I missed the memo there and just started pounding them down. After the first plateful I was up having 7 compared to “The Juggler’s” 5 and the other two with 6 a piece. I’m feeling pretty good at this point, the oysters don’t even taste that bad, only a little bad.

The next plateful comes and I lock in and eat 10 while “The Juggler” has 2 and the others have 6 each. The current leader board is

Me: 17

“The Mosher” and “That’s nuts”: 12

“The Juggler”: 7

At this point though the taste of the oysters really starts to set in and I ask myself why such dogshit food exists in this world.

I got through a few more oysters but the results certainly aren’t pretty. Each one becomes more painful than the last. My eyes bulge out, my face tears up, my hands cover my mouth to prevent anything from coming out. It’s a symphony of suffering. Eventually it gets to the point that I start gagging when I even have to smell oysters and that’s when I know that I have to drop out.

I thought for sure I could win. But sadly no. Everyone else keeps going and I stop paying as much attention to the exact splits but I can tell you that “The Mosher” and “That’s nuts” really picked up steam.

In the end the final scores are

“The Mosher”: 34

“That’s nuts”: 32

Me and “The Juggler”: 21

In the end “The Mosher” won! I think from my perspective he was definitely the dark horse he really stepped yp. And of course I learned that I could not beat “That’s nuts” at her own oyster eating game. An unfortunate result sure but on the plus side I more than tripled my oyster eating PR from 6 to 21. Also I think aside from the last 3 or 4 I was able to nosell eating oysters. And I tied with “The Juggler” which feels like an accomplishment in its own right!

“That’s nuts” thought I would only be able to eat a low teens number so apparently I surpassed her expectations as well.

My knees are buckling a bit right now. And I don’t know everything is really funny.

We wrapped up at the bar and I walked “The Philosopher” home. I was desperately craving to be in bed and go to sleep so I could wake up refreshed. It took all I had to muster the energy to call the Waymo and shuffle up to my apartment.

All things considered it was quite a fun experience. I wonder if I can leverage how I acted there and pick up that persona during an intense bar night. Maybe I can flip a switch and be that on command. I certainly enjoyed being that off the walls happy and upbeat.

Saturday

I met up with “Not a flosst” cause to volunteer at a plant a tree event. Unfortunately the organizers never sent her the place to meet so we wound up walking around Japantown for a while and caught up which was fun in its own right.

Afterwards I went to the zoo with “This little piggie didn’t go to the slaughter house,” "The Fisherwoman,” “The Quant,” “The Raja of Rhythm,” “The Yogi” and a couple others.

“This little piggie didn’t go the slaughterhouse” now works at the zoo and offered to give us all a free tour which was awesome!

We got to see a lot of different animals including red pandas, black bears, grizzly bears, blue and gold Beatles, bugs that look like sticks, and a lot more!

Huge props to “This little piggie didn’t go to the slaughterhouse” for such a fun time!

She works on a wide variety of projects at the zoo including how to get money from millionaires to fund a giant panda exhibit and planning how to restructure the zoo and move things around.

Some random tidbits I learned: it’s a code red when the lions or the black widow escape but a common occurrence when the lemurs do, many penguins thrive in temperature similar to ours, and turtle gender is determined by the temperature of the eggs before they are born.

Apparently the SF Zoo has a program where you can donate to be a VIP! I took a dollar out of my wallet and gave it to “This little piggie didn’t go to the slaughter market” but she said I was $19,999 short. $20,000 to be a VIP! Forget the lions and the bears, I learned who the real predators are here!

From there me, “The Quant,” “The Raja of Rhythm,” and “The Yogi” went to a BBQ hosted by “The Italian Stallion”. He made some really good brisket which I quite enjoyed, and his roommate made some banana bread that was awesome!

Sunday

I spent the morning walking around leisurely enjoying Golden Gate Park and Twin peaks. It certainly wasn’t novel but it was the first time in a while I really just spent a morning walking around myself which was quite nice.

Afterwards I headed to Lake Merritt for “Not Misinformed’s” birthday! When I got there I wasn’t quite sure which side of Lake Merritt I was supposed to meet at so I chilled by the south side for a while. I got a call from “The Foxy lady” saying we were supposed to meet at the Northside so I sprinted over

The birthday party was a lot of fun! “Not misinformed” is big into cooking and baking so he came up with a Chopped theme birthday where people made food using different mystery ingredients. I unfortunately didn’t make any food because I admittedly forgot but thankfully there was enough food to go around.

The best dish for a special bedazzled chefs hat courtesy of “The Foxy lady” which was quite nice! The winner was this one person who made these really good sweet potato’s fudge brownies that were also dairy free which SLAPPED.

While I didn’t make a meal, I did win best birthday card by being the only one to make a custom birthday card

At the party I met quite a few interesting people! One guy I met is doing a PhD in conspiracy theories and apparently he was asked by a democratic senator if he could build a good version of QAnon. Suffice to say that hadn’t happened yet.

I also met someone who is doing a PhD in Symplectic Geometry. If you’re like me and have no idea what that means, the way she described it is to first think of pairs of attributes that are tracked together like position and momentum in classical mechanics. Now think instead of a pair what if you have a triplet or 4-let (essentially 3d and 4d). She studied whole spaces in 3d and 4d which is insane! Maybe she can help me find a manifold in which my doodles actually look good! Also apparently she said she will find this episode by way of “Thr Foxy lady” so let me know through her what you think.

On a side note I appreciate “The Foxy lady” being my hype man amongst her friends as a good bunch of them have already heard of me and the newsletter which tickles me.

Finally the birthday cake came out. I jokingly asked “The Foxy lady” if we could do the thing where we shove “Not Misinformed’s” face in the cake. She said no but that next year she would be interested in having her face shoved in the cake. And she will be doubly impressed if I remember so I’m writing this here as a chronicle of the promise. I hope you’re ready “The Foxy lady”!
